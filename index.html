Safety & Contraindications

Please consult a healthcare professional before starting this training if you have:

  • Serious or unstable heart or cardiovascular conditions
  • Severe or uncontrolled respiratory conditions
  • Uncontrolled high or very low blood pressure
  • A recent heart attack, stroke, or major surgery
  • A history of fainting or loss of consciousness during breathing exercises
  • Epilepsy or a seizure disorder, particularly if the exercises include breath-holding
  • Pregnancy, particularly if the exercises include prolonged breath-holding
  • Any medical condition for which your healthcare provider has advised you to avoid breathing exercises or breath-holding

Perform all exercises gently and never force your breathing or hold your breath beyond a comfortable level.

Stop immediately if you experience chest pain, severe shortness of breath, faintness, significant dizziness, unusual heart palpitations, confusion, or any sudden worsening of symptoms.

This training is intended for educational and general wellness purposes only and is not a substitute for medical diagnosis, treatment, or individualized rehabilitation.
